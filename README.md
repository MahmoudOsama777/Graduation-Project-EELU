## 5. Project Idea: Student Housing Marketplace

### 5.1. Project Overview

The **Student Housing Marketplace** is a web platform designed to connect university students looking for accommodation with landlords offering student housing.

The platform acts as an intermediary between students and landlords, making it easier to discover suitable accommodation, compare available options, evaluate housing conditions, and manage booking requests.

By combining advanced search capabilities, AI-powered recommendations, student matching, and a structured booking system, the platform aims to make finding student accommodation easier, more transparent, and more convenient.

### 5.2. Core Features

### 1. Landlord & Housing Listings
Landlords can add housing details including location, price, transportation, rooms, student capacity, room capacity, internet speed, services, photos, gender preference, description, and availability.

### 2. Student Profile
Students create profiles with their age, university, college, academic year, budget, room preference, maximum distance, internet preference, quietness preference, and transportation needs.

### 3. Search & Filters
Students can search by price, distance, internet speed, room capacity, number of students, transportation, university, college, and available rooms, with sorting options such as nearest, cheapest, fastest internet, and most available.

### 4. AI Housing Recommendation
AI analyzes the student's preferences, including price, distance, internet, and transportation, then recommends suitable housing and explains **why** each option matches.

### 5. AI Housing Assistant
Students can ask natural-language questions such as:
- “I’m an Engineering student with a 4,000 EGP budget and need good internet near the university.”
- “What is the closest housing to University X?”
- “How long will it take me to reach my lecture from this housing?”

### 6. Reviews & Ratings
Students can rate housing, internet quality, transportation, and landlords.

### 7. Verification
Landlords provide ID, proof of ownership/rental authorization, and phone verification. Verified landlords and housing are clearly identified.

### 8. Booking System
**Student → Booking Request → Landlord Accepts/Rejects → Student Confirmation → Booking Confirmed**

The system also manages booking status, cancellation, check-in, and check-out dates.

### 9. Notifications
Users receive notifications for booking requests, approvals, cancellations, and new housing listings that match their preferences.

## 6. Project Objectives

The project aims to:

- **Simplify Housing Search:** Help students find accommodation that matches their needs and budget.
- **Improve Decision-Making:** Provide detailed listings, reviews, and comparisons.
- **Use AI Effectively:** Deliver personalized housing recommendations and roommate matching.
- **Increase Trust:** Introduce verification procedures and transparent reviews.
- **Streamline Bookings:** Organize the interaction between students and landlords.
- **Apply Technical Skills:** Gain practical experience in full-stack development, database design, and AI integration.
- **Strengthen Teamwork:** Encourage collaboration, shared responsibility, and professional software development practices.
